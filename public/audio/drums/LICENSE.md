# Acoustic drum samples

The kick, snare, and closed hi-hat samples in this directory are adapted from
the **Virtuosity Drums** sample library by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer
Samples. They are distributed under the **CC0 1.0 Universal** public-domain
dedication.

Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums

These browser-ready AAC-LC variants were curated by the Keyboardia project
from the same-microphone velocity layers. The filenames were shortened for
use in this app; the audio content is otherwise unchanged.

Keyboardia provenance manifest:
https://github.com/adewale/keyboardia/blob/main/app/public/instruments/LICENSE.md
