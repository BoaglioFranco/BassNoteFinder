# Electric bass samples

`finger-e1.wav` and `picked-e1.wav` are from **Bass Guitar YR**, recorded by
Andrea Biasior from a Yamaha RBX bass guitar and published through FreePats.

- Source: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/ElectricGuitar/clean-electric-bass.html
- Release: 2019-09-30
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The source recordings are dedicated to the public domain. Filenames were
changed locally to identify their articulation and pitch.
